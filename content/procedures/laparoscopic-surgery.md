overview: "Minimal access, or laparoscopic, surgery uses small incisions, a camera, and fine instruments in place of a large traditional opening. In children this approach is used for conditions ranging from appendicitis and gallbladder disease to complex intra-abdominal procedures, offering less postoperative pain, smaller scars, and typically a faster return to normal activity."
procedure_text: "Carbon dioxide gas is used to create working space within the abdomen, and the operation is completed through a small number of 3–12mm incisions using a camera and specialised instruments. Depending on the underlying condition, conversion to an open approach may occasionally be needed for safety."
