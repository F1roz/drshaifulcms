overview: "Neuroblastoma is one of the more common solid tumours of childhood, arising from developing nerve cells and most frequently found in the adrenal gland or along the sympathetic nerve chain in the abdomen or chest. Management is coordinated by a multidisciplinary team of paediatric surgical, medical, and radiation oncology specialists, following internationally developed treatment protocols."
procedure_text: "Surgery may involve biopsy for diagnosis and risk stratification, or complete tumour resection once any preceding chemotherapy has reduced tumour size and risk to surrounding structures."
prep: "Comprehensive staging investigations and multidisciplinary team discussion precede any surgical intervention."
recovery: "Recovery and further treatment planning are guided by the multidisciplinary oncology team based on surgical and pathological findings."
complications: "Risks relate to the tumour's proximity to major blood vessels and organs and are discussed individually."
followup: "Long-term oncological follow-up is coordinated with the paediatric oncology team."
