title: "Pre-auricular Sinus & Skin Tag Excision"
category: "General Paediatric Surgery"
catid: "general"
summary: "Removal of small pits or skin tags near the ear that are present from birth."
image: "/assets/img/img1.jpg"
overview: "A pre-auricular sinus is a small, blind-ending pit in front of the ear formed during early ear development; it does not affect hearing but can become infected. Pre-auricular skin tags are separate fleshy nodules with no connection to a pit and carry no infection risk, being purely a cosmetic consideration."
procedure_text: "Asymptomatic sinuses can simply be observed. Infected sinuses are treated with antibiotics first, with surgical removal once settled. Surgery involves an elliptical incision around the opening; for sinuses, the full tract is traced down to the ear cartilage before complete removal."
prep: "Routine day-surgery fasting guidelines apply for surgical cases."
recovery: "Day-case surgery; dissolvable sutures and simple wound care over one to two weeks."
complications: "Low risk of infection, bleeding, or incomplete removal requiring further surgery."
followup: "Review at two to three weeks."
order: 14
