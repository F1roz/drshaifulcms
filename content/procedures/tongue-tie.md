---
title: "Tongue-Tie Division"
category: "General Paediatric Surgery"
catid: "general"
summary: "Release of a tight band of tissue under the tongue that is restricting movement or feeding."
image: "/assets/img/img1.jpg"
overview: "A tongue-tie (short lingual frenulum) is common and does not always need treatment — division is considered only when it is genuinely restricting feeding, speech, or oral function."
procedure_text: "A quick procedure performed with local anaesthetic; the tight band is divided using electrical cautery, which also controls any bleeding at the same time."
prep: "Minimal preparation; discussed at consultation."
recovery: "Children typically return to school or day care as soon as they are comfortable, often the same day."
complications: "Very low complication rate; mild discomfort and a small ulcer under the tongue during healing is normal."
followup: "Reviewed as needed; most children require no further visit."
order: 16
