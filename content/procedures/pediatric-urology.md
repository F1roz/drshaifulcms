overview: "Paediatric urology covers a broad range of conditions including vesicoureteric reflux (backward flow of urine toward the kidney), hypospadias (an abnormally positioned urethral opening), pelvi-ureteric junction obstruction, and other structural conditions of the kidney, bladder, and genitalia identified either before birth, in infancy, or in later childhood."
procedure_text: "Treatment ranges from minimally invasive endoscopic techniques to open or laparoscopic reconstructive surgery, selected according to the specific diagnosis, its severity, and the child's age."
prep: "Pre-operative imaging (such as ultrasound or specialised urinary tract studies) guides planning; fasting instructions are provided before admission."
recovery: "Recovery varies by procedure; some operations are day-case, while reconstructive surgery may require a short hospital stay with catheter care."
complications: "Risks vary by procedure and are discussed individually with families before surgery."
followup: "Follow-up typically includes clinical review and, where relevant, repeat imaging to confirm a good outcome."
