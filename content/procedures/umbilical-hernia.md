summary: "Closure of a defect at the belly button, more common in infants born prematurely."
image: "/assets/img/img1.jpg"
overview: "An umbilical hernia is a soft bulge at the navel caused by incomplete closure of the abdominal wall opening through which the umbilical cord once passed. Most close spontaneously by around four years of age; surgery is considered for hernias that persist beyond this, are unusually large, or cause discomfort."
procedure_text: "A short day-case operation under general anaesthesia, typically taking around twenty minutes, in which the defect is closed with dissolvable sutures."
prep: "Routine day-surgery fasting guidelines apply."
recovery: "Most children resume normal activity within a few days; heavy activity is avoided for two weeks."
complications: "Low overall risk profile; a small chance of wound infection, bleeding, or recurrence."
followup: "Review at three to four weeks."
