---
title: "Mucocoele of the Lip Excision"
category: "General Paediatric Surgery"
catid: "general"
summary: "Removal of a small fluid-filled swelling on the inner lip caused by a blocked salivary gland."
image: "/assets/img/img1.jpg"
overview: "A mucocoele forms when a minor salivary gland duct in the lip is injured (often from lip-biting) and saliva collects under the surface, forming a soft bluish swelling."
procedure_text: "Short day-case excision under general anaesthesia, with the affected gland tissue removed to reduce the chance of recurrence."
prep: "Routine day-surgery fasting guidelines apply."
recovery: "Paracetamol is usually sufficient for comfort in the first day; lip swelling and bruising are normal and settle within about a week. A normal diet can resume once comfortable, though vomiting on the day of surgery is common from the anaesthetic."
complications: "Small risk of infection, requiring antibiotics if it develops; low risk of recurrence."
followup: "Review at two to three weeks."
order: 10
