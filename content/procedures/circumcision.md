summary: "Surgical removal of the foreskin, performed for medical or religious indications under general anaesthesia."
image: "/assets/img/img1.jpg"
overview: "Circumcision is one of the most commonly performed procedures in paediatric surgical practice. It may be recommended for recurrent balanitis, difficulty passing urine due to a tight foreskin (phimosis), recurrent urinary tract infections, or by family choice."
procedure_text: "The operation is carried out under general anaesthesia, usually delivered by face mask, with a small intravenous line placed in the hand or arm. The foreskin is carefully excised and the wound closed with dissolvable sutures. The procedure typically takes 20–30 minutes and is performed as day surgery."
prep: "Fasting instructions will be provided before the day of surgery. Please bring any regular medications and inform the team of allergies."
recovery: "Bathing is safe from the day after surgery. Antibiotic cream is applied two to three times daily, and a barrier ointment is used with each nappy change to prevent sticking. Healing of the glans takes around two weeks; redness, crusting, and mild discharge during this period are expected."
complications: "Contact the clinic promptly if there is thick yellow discharge, worsening pain, spreading redness, fever, or steady bleeding, which may indicate infection or a wound problem. Rarely, revision surgery is needed for cosmetic asymmetry."
followup: "A routine review is arranged two to three weeks after surgery."
