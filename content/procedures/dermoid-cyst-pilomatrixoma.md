title: "Dermoid Cysts & Pilomatrixoma Excision"
category: "General Paediatric Surgery"
catid: "general"
summary: "Removal of benign skin and soft-tissue lumps commonly found on the face, scalp, or neck in children."
image: "/assets/img/img1.jpg"
overview: "Dermoid cysts arise from skin cells trapped beneath the surface during early development, typically appearing near the outer eyebrow or in the midline of the neck; they enlarge slowly and can become infected if injured. Pilomatrixomas are firm, benign lumps arising from hair-follicle cells, usually on the face or neck, and are occasionally mildly tender."
procedure_text: "Both conditions are treated with straightforward surgical excision under general anaesthesia — the lump is removed in full through a small, carefully placed incision, closed with dissolvable sutures and skin glue. The operation generally takes around twenty minutes."
prep: "Routine day-surgery fasting guidelines apply."
recovery: "Day-case surgery; the glue dressing falls away naturally over one to two weeks."
complications: "Low risk of infection, bleeding, or (rarely) recurrence if any cyst wall is left behind."
followup: "Review at three to four weeks, with discussion of pathology results if relevant."
order: 2
