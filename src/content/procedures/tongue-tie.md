<h2>Overview</h2>
      <p>A tongue-tie (short lingual frenulum) is common and does not always need treatment — division is considered only when it is genuinely restricting feeding, speech, or oral function.</p>
      <h2>The Procedure</h2>
      <p>A quick procedure performed with local anaesthetic; the tight band is divided using electrical cautery, which also controls any bleeding at the same time.</p>
      <h2>Preparing for Surgery</h2>
      <p>Minimal preparation; discussed at consultation.</p>
      <h2>Recovery</h2>
      <p>Children typically return to school or day care as soon as they are comfortable, often the same day.</p>
      <h2>Possible Complications</h2>
      <p>Very low complication rate; mild discomfort and a small ulcer under the tongue during healing is normal.</p>
      <h2>Follow-up</h2>
      <p>Reviewed as needed; most children require no further visit.</p>
